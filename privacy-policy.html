Privacy Policy

Short version: I don't sell, share, or hand your data to anyone. It's used only to reply to you. Here's the full detail.

What This Covers

This policy applies to this portfolio site (vkdeveloper900.github.io) and its contact form. It explains what information is collected when you use the site, why, and what happens to it afterward.

Information I Collect

When you submit the contact form, I collect:

  • Your name and email address, as you enter them
  • The message you write
  • Your IP address and browser/device information (user agent), captured automatically for spam and abuse prevention

I don't use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising scripts anywhere on this site. Browsing the site without submitting the form doesn't send me any personal data.

How I Use It

Your contact form submission is used for exactly one purpose: replying to your inquiry. That includes an automatic "thanks for reaching out" confirmation email, a notification to me so I know to follow up, and any direct reply I send you afterward. Your message is stored so I can track leads and refer back to past conversations if you reach out again — nothing more.

Data Sharing

I don't sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any third party. It isn't used for marketing lists, newsletters, or anything beyond replying to you.

Where It's Stored

Submissions are stored in a private database on my backend server. Emails are sent through a standard SMTP mail provider. Only I have access to this data.

Third-Party Services

This site loads fonts (Google Fonts) and icons (Font Awesome, Bootstrap Icons) from their respective CDNs to render properly. These providers may see basic request data (like your IP address) as a normal part of serving those files, the same as any website asset — I don't share any contact form data with them.

Your Rights

You can ask me to delete any information you've submitted, or ask what I have on file, at any time — just email me using the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: vksuthar900@gmail.com
